# Account Switcher

**Switch between your Geometry Dash accounts seamlessly!**

---

### About

This mod allows you to store <cl>multiple accounts</c> locally and switch between them dynamically without needing to re-enter your account login.

To use this mod, go to the <co>Account Settings</c> and under <cg>'More'</c>, you will find the new <cg>'Switch Account'</c> button. From there, you can add your current account to the list and switch between stored accounts with ease.

<cy>![❗](frame:GJ_infoIcon_001.png?scale=0.55) This won't load your account data when switching accounts, so you need to load your data from the cloud manually after switching.</c>

---

### Disclaimer

<cr>By using this mod, you acknowledge that your **GJP2 (encrypted password)** will be used in this mod and is stored locally (on your device) and will be used to log in when switching accounts. If you do not wish to store this password anywhere, **Do not use this mod**.</c>
