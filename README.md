# Account Switcher

**Switch between your Geometry Dash accounts seemlessly!**

---

### About

This mod allows you to store <cl>multiple accounts</c> locally and switch between them dynamically without needing to re-enter your account login.

<co>To use this mod, go to the Account Settings and under <cg>'More'</c>, you will find the new <cg>'Switch Account'</c> button. From there, you can add your current account to the list and switch between stored accounts with ease.</c>

<cy>*![❗](frame:GJ_infoIcon_001.png?scale=0.55) This won't load your account data when switching accounts, you need to load your data from the cloud manually after switching.*</c>

### Disclaimer

### <cr>![❗](frame:info-alert.png?scale=0.45) By using this mod, you acknowledge that your **GJP2 (encrypted password)** will be used in this mod and are stored locally and used to log in when switching accounts. If you do not wish to store this password anywhere, **Do not use this mod**.</c>
